# App Store Screenshot Brief — PlanCraft AI

## Positioning
- Own the category: voice-first AI execution.
- Do not lead with: generic AI planner, to-do app, or broad productivity language.
- Core promise: speak once, then let the app organize, schedule, and follow through.

## Screenshot Flow
1. **Plan your whole day in 10 seconds**
   Show the voice capture flow with transcript plus structured tasks.
   Use a screen from the voice planner or task planner dialog.
   Goal: make the product different on the first frame.

2. **Just speak. AI organizes everything.**
   Show the before/after conversion from messy input to clear tasks, dates, and priorities.
   Use a screen where AI has already parsed the task details.
   Goal: prove this is more than note-taking.

3. **Your day, auto-organized**
   Show the timeline, today view, or planner overview with visible ordering.
   Use a screen that shows now/next/later structure instead of a generic list.
   Goal: sell the outcome, not the mechanism.

4. **Reminders that actually reach you**
   Show channel selection or reminder settings with WhatsApp, email, push, SMS, and call options.
   Use the quick setup or settings flow.
   Goal: highlight the strongest functional differentiator.

5. **Calendar-aware all day**
   Show Google Calendar sync, meeting prep, or event-linked planning.
   Use the calendar integration or a schedule screen that visibly connects work and meetings.
   Goal: show that execution is connected to real life, not trapped in a task list.

6. **Built for busy professionals**
   Show the cleanest high-trust screen: planner overview, dashboard, or a polished home state.
   Use this slot for composure and credibility, not feature overload.
   Goal: close with identity and trust until stronger social proof is available.

## Copy Rules
- Keep each headline to one short sentence.
- Keep supporting text to one line max.
- Put the user outcome first and the word "AI" second.
- Use real UI, not abstract shapes pretending to be product.
- Avoid copy that makes the user translate what the app does.

## Claim Guardrails
- Do not use fake social proof, review scores, or press quotes.
- Do not promise automatic rescheduling unless that exact flow is shipped and stable.
- Do not lead with collaboration unless the release is targeting team workflows first.
- Do not mention Google sign-in in mobile screenshots while it is disabled in packaged apps.

## Recommended Supporting Lines
- Screen 1: "Speak once. AI turns it into tasks, time, and reminders."
- Screen 2: "Brain-dumps become clear tasks, dates, and priorities."
- Screen 3: "See what to do now, next, and later in one timeline."
- Screen 4: "Push, WhatsApp, email, SMS, and voice call."
- Screen 5: "Sync your calendar and prep without double entry."
- Screen 6: "Voice-first planning for overloaded schedules."
