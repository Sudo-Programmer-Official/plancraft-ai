# App Store Screenshot Brief — PlanCraft AI

## Positioning
- Own the category: voice-first AI execution.
- Lead with outcomes, not features.
- Core promise: stop overthinking, say what you need, and let the app turn it into a plan.

## Screenshot Flow
1. **Stop Overthinking Your Day**
   Supporting line: Plan It in Seconds with AI.
   Show the fastest voice-to-plan moment.
   Goal: win the install decision on the first frame.

2. **Just Say What You Need**
   Supporting line: We Turn It Into a Plan.
   Show messy input becoming structured tasks, dates, and next steps.
   Goal: prove the product removes planning friction.

3. **Your System. Your Rules.**
   Supporting line: Fully Customizable Planning.
   Show a view where the user can shape the plan, not just accept AI output.
   Goal: position the product as flexible instead of rigid.

4. **Your Data. Fully Private.**
   Supporting line: No Noise. No Distractions.
   Show the cleanest high-trust UI state.
   Goal: build trust and lower privacy resistance.

5. **Never Miss Anything**
   Supporting line: Smart Reminders Built-In.
   Show reminder settings or channel options with visible value.
   Goal: highlight the strongest utility differentiator.

6. **Start Planning Smarter Today**
   Show the most polished overview screen with visible momentum.
   Goal: close with confidence and a simple install CTA.

## Copy Rules
- Keep each screen to one message.
- Keep each headline to one short sentence.
- Keep supporting text to one line max.
- Put the user outcome first and the word "AI" second.
- Use real UI, not abstract shapes pretending to be product.
- Avoid copy that makes the user translate what the app does.
- Use pain-to-solution language when possible.

## Remove From Current Screens
- Do not use generic lines like "Clean UI" or "Simple."
- Do not dump multiple features into one screenshot.
- Do not use small text that disappears at store size.

## Design Rules
- Big, bold headline at the top.
- Keep the product UI centered and readable.
- Maintain strong contrast and the existing purple brand direction.
- Do not overload any single frame with multiple stories.
- Make every frame understandable in under two seconds.

## Emotional Tension
- Prefer "Stop Wasting Time Planning" over "AI Plans Everything."
- Prefer pain-to-solution phrasing over feature labeling.
- Make the user feel the before state, then show the relief.

## Claim Guardrails
- Do not use fake social proof, review scores, or press quotes.
- Do not promise automatic rescheduling unless that exact flow is shipped and stable.
- Do not lead with collaboration unless the release is targeting team workflows first.
- Do not mention Google sign-in in mobile screenshots while it is disabled in packaged apps.
